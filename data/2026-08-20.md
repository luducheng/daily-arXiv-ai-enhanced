### [1] [Asteroseismic analysis of red giants in eclipsing binaries using two methods: implications for scaling relations and chemical composition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18250)
*M. Yıldız,S. Örtel,T. Çakır Alsaç*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: This paper studies solar-like oscillating red giants in eclipsing binaries, combining dynamical mass/radius measurements with asteroseismic constraints. The authors apply two methods to estimate initial chemical composition (one varying initial helium, the other varying initial metallicity with a helium-metallicity relation), construct interior models for 11 EBs, and obtain coeval solutions for eight systems. Method: Two methods are applied to estimate initial chemical composition: Method I treats initial helium abundance (Y0) as a free parameter, while Method II treats initial metallicity (Z0) as a free parameter assuming a Y0-Z0 relation.
